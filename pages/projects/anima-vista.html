Anima-Vista

Anima-Vista is a web application that provides users with a platform for memory preservation and retrieval. I came up with the theoretical concept of being able to take a dream and turn it into a visual representation, and then being able to share that with others, fostering a community of "dreamweavers" to be able to converse in deep-rooted concepts.
